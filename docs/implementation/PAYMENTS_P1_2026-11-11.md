**Status:** deployed and verified in production, inert (flag off). Release 78658e5, ledger **269** (`20261110180000`), deployment dpl_HgiuTp9ku3ZRG8XvW24nxrn42htS.

## What shipped

- Owner decisions recorded: pay-after-accept, Stripe/Kinnso/HKD, 15% monthly payouts, default cancellation/dispute policy, creator flat prices, Kinnso owns the Stripe account/KYC.
- `guides.price_minor` with owner-only set-price RPC/API (HKD minor units, 1.00–100,000.00, nullable).
- Booking lifecycle statuses (`awaiting_payment`, `paid`, `payment_expired`, `refunded`).
- `booking_payments`, `payment_events` (append-only, unique provider event id), `creator_payouts`; service-role only.
- Accepting a priced guide under the flag creates the obligation (amount, 15% snapshot, 7-day deadline); internal mark-paid hook; expiry sweep; audited ops refund for the fake provider; completion covers paid trips.
- Default-off `payments` flag with ops toggle/status; production stays off.
