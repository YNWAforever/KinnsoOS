# Phase 6 P3 — signed provider webhook, idempotent ledger, paid notifications

**Status:** deployed and verified in production, inert (flag off, no provider secret). Release `5d30843`, ledger **272** (`20261111140000`), deployment `dpl_9cFVPSqmkXCJ9vBhucnaSTPN5SjG`.

## What shipped

- **Signed webhook entry point** (`POST /api/payments/webhook`): verifies a Stripe-style HMAC-SHA256 signature over the raw body (`t=<unix>,v1=<hex>`, 5-minute tolerance, multiple candidate signatures, constant-time compare, 64 KiB body cap) before parsing anything. Tampered, stale, wrongly-signed or unsigned requests are refused; nothing reaches the database on failure. With no `KINNSO_PAYMENTS_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (production today) the endpoint answers `503 not_configured` and touches nothing.
- **Idempotent ledger**: `public.kinnso_payment_webhook_ingest` is a service-role-only RPC that validates the reduced event, records it under the unique `payment_events.provider_event_id`, and applies at most one paid transition. Replays return `replayed: true` without a second event or notice.
- **Paid transition** (`kinnso_internal.mark_payment_paid`, now replay-safe and three-arg): flips the payment to `paid`, records the provider intent, moves the booking to `paid`, and in the same transaction writes the traveller `booking.paid` and creator `booking.payment_received` inbox notices and the matching email outbox rows (when the outbox flag is on). Muted recipients are skipped by the same rules as every other booking notice.
- **Safety checks before confirming**: a provider-reported `amount_total` or currency that differs from the server-side snapshot is audited as ignored and never confirms the trip; events for unknown payments are recorded without creating rows; late events for expired/refunded rows are audited as `not_payable` instead of resurrecting them.
- **Widened vocabularies**: inbox events, notification mutes and the email outbox now accept the two paid notice types; the mute settings response offers seven types; `kinnso_get_notification_mutes` / `kinnso_set_notification_mute` accept them; ops payment status reports `providerEvents`.
- **Copy**: bilingual paid templates ("付款完成，旅程已確認" / "Payment received; your trip is confirmed"), creator counterpart, amount line rendered from integer minor units, and inbox/mute labels for both types. `.env.example` documents the provider seam and webhook secret.

## Limits

- Only the fake provider is exercised end to end. The Stripe signature format and hosted-checkout request are implemented and unit-tested, but no real Stripe account has been touched; creating the live endpoint/webhook secret is P5 (account, KYC, Vercel env).
- Expired/failed provider events are audit-only: the 7-day deadline sweep remains the authority, and a genuinely late successful payment is left for ops reconciliation rather than auto-confirming an expired trip.
- No refund webhooks, receipts/PDFs, payout accrual or creator transfers yet (refunds remain fake-only; payout tables are schema-only until P4/P5).
- Email delivery still requires a configured email provider; P3 only queues the outbox rows (the local acceptance enables the outbox flag to prove the rows are written).
- Enabling live payments requires both `KINNSO_PAYMENTS_PROVIDER=stripe` with `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `KINNSO_PAYMENTS_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; without them checkout and webhook stay inert by design.
